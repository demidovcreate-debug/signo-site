Privacy Policy

Signo: UK Road Signs ("the app"). Effective 11 November 2026.

The app helps you learn British road signs and English driving vocabulary. It is published by Roman Demidov ("we", "us"). You can contact us at roman4apps@gmail.com.

In short

The app works on your device. It has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell personal data. The only time information leaves your device is when you buy paid content (see "Purchases").

What stays on your device

To remember your progress the app stores, on your device only: your chosen language and learning mode, which signs and cards you have marked as known, your lesson and quiz results, and whether you have seen the introduction hints. We cannot see this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Some content is unlocked by an in-app purchase. Payment is handled by Apple; we never receive your card or bank details. To check and restore your purchases we use RevenueCat, a service that manages in-app purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated for the app on your device, your purchase and subscription status, and technical information needed to provide the service, such as device type, operating system version and IP address. It does not receive your name or email address from the app. See the RevenueCat privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Pronunciation

When you tap the speaker button, the English text of a card is read aloud by your device's built-in speech engine. The app does not send this text to us or to any other company.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to improve the app. We keep them for as long as needed for that purpose and do not share them with others.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to or restrict how we use it. Because progress data stays on your device, you can remove it at any time by deleting the app. For purchase data held by RevenueCat or Apple, contact us or the company concerned. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
