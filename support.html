Contact us

Email roman4apps@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use and describe what happened. We read every message.

In the app, tap Report a problem or suggest an idea at the bottom of the home screen. It opens an email to us with your language and device details already filled in, so you only need to describe the problem or idea.

Common questions

I bought paid content but it is locked. Open the app, go to the purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I change the language? Tap the language button on the home screen and pick a language. Content translations are machine drafts that have not yet been reviewed by native speakers; tell us if you find a mistake.

I found a mistake in a sign name or meaning. Email us the sign and what is wrong. Sign names and meanings follow the Highway Code and the Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 2016.

Where is my progress stored? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.
